# Rabalata Rides – 60-second advert script

**Style:** realistic South African city and suburban shuttle footage, natural daylight, confident human voice-over, black/yellow/white graphic overlays. Use licensed footage and real approved vehicles/drivers only.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over / on-screen words |
|---|---|---|
| 0–7 sec | Morning city traffic, passenger checking phone | “Every day, every journey is different. Your ride should work around you.” |
| 7–15 sec | Rabalata Rides app branding and booking screen | “Meet Rabalata Rides. Request a shuttle from your pickup point to your destination.” |
| 15–24 sec | Map route and fare offer screen | “Choose your route. Suggest your fare. Drivers can accept or make an offer that works for both of you.” |
| 24–34 sec | Driver profile, vehicle, plate and driver portrait | “Know which driver is coming, see the vehicle details and follow the trip on the map.” |
| 34–43 sec | Passenger and driver greeting beside vehicle | “From the first request to the final destination, stay informed along the way.” |
| 43–51 sec | Cash and secure card payment graphics | “Choose cash or secure card payment, with clear trip and payment records.” |
| 51–57 sec | City road footage and brand logo | “Rabalata Rides. Your journey. Your choice. Your ride.” |
| 57–60 sec | Logo, website address placeholder and call to action | “Get ready to ride. Rabalata Rides.” |

**Production note:** Do not claim driver vetting, instant withdrawals, real-time tracking or payment protection until those functions have passed launch testing.
